# Integrated art pass 1

The first real rendition adds original world paintings, material-specific walking lips and recessed corbels, authored pear/penitent/boss artwork, non-colliding final-boss manifestation and a compact HUD. It changes presentation only. The ten-camera sample is unchanged, including the initially remote final-boss chamber: the new distant manifestation addresses the absence instead of moving the benchmark camera.

No generated comparison poster is used as an asset or evidence. Both erroneous generator outputs are excluded. Original asset source is Tools/generate_visual_v6.py. More drawing detail is not itself proof of superiority; actual pass 1 captures must be reviewed.

Read-only visual components mirror existing renderer enabled/alpha states. Geometry and moving joints remain the original simulation. Corbels and the distant boss manifestation are rendered behind physical geometry and never acquire colliders. Target risks to check: tiling/stretching; phase alpha visibility; corbel overhang ambiguity; new HUD/controls overlap; white-world contrast; final boss representation versus its real collision target.
