5. Build, capture the same fixture set, grade, commit. Inspect defects at full size and thumbnail size. Repeat; do not move the sample set to hide weak cases.
6. Keep comparison superiority as an honest manual gate. Subjective evidence does not become an objective universal claim. Preserve the user's target even if an iteration still falls short.
7. No physics/stat/level simplification and no Nintendo art in the runtime. No fake generated screenshots, and no paid external agents.

Score anchors per dimension (0-5): 0 absent/broken, 1 prototype, 2 coherent but visibly crude, 3 solid and legible, 4 distinctive polished craft, 5 exceptional resolved craft. A tie is not superiority. The four dimensions are style/world identity; character/silhouette; gameplay clarity; polish/pixel/material coherence.
