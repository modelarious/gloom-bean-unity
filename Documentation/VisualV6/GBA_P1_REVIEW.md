# Native GBA pass 1 review

Source66322265, native20 screenshots: ten original camera fixtures plus ten clearly prefixed GBA framing variants. Every captured 1200x800 viewport consists of uniform5x5 blocks: actual240x160 world and bitmap HUD. No production collider was added. Real captures are preserved alongside the failed first compile.

Compared with4424d5c, the fixed-camera scenes have visibly crisper lettering, contact edges and bounded pixel clusters. The smaller HUD uses20/160 pixels instead of desktop heading blocks and frees the lower-right scene. This is genuine improvement, not a superiority certificate.

Defects identified from the captures: (1) the tighter Mirror crop cuts off the real body, and the final-boss framing places the Host behind the footer; keep the original witnesses and use a dual-body-appropriate lens/new labelled center, not delete examples. (2) Purple/green palette hierarchy remains muddy in Orchard/Fall; the opening smiling facades remain generic unarticulated green shapes. (3) HUD simplification hides conserved Wax volume, polarity and Ink budget; show compact state, not just form name. (4) Castle contact surfaces look crisp but too mechanically repetitive. (5) Cathedral still reuses the same backdrop as Rain; give the cathedral an architectural rather than open-shaft interior. (6) GBA UI needs a real keyboard/menu review for wrapping and disabled-selection visibility.

The four subjective dimensions remain below or tied with the WL4 benchmarks. In particular hand-authored scene composition and animation expressiveness are not solved by resolution reduction. Next pass addresses the measured defects and reruns the same original ten cameras as well as labelled handheld variants.
