## Bosses and endings

The Usher tests delayed occupancy, pendulum interaction and husk placement; the Judge tests conserved mass, terrain support and roots; the Surveyor tests mirrored, interior and depth relationships. The Weight of Everyone finishes only after actual load redirection and horizontal bracing send its mass to the bottom. The Host of Hosts steals a selected tenant and exposes five different pair solutions. Final victory is tied to the released physical heart, not to a test writing a win flag.

All five final pair families have separate native witnesses: Lodestone+Shadow, Echo+Ink, Wax+Gullet, Stitch+Coffin and Mirror+Parallax. The complete earned campaign is exercised at zero, nineteen and twenty Mercy boundaries. Restoration affects the final presentation only, never erases the persistent first corruption, and is unavailable through Practice.

## Engineering interpretations and honest limits

The atlas pages are schematic design pitches rather than tile-by-tile production maps. Builders implement their systemic puzzles with original geometry. Moving machines have authored constraints; this is not an unrestricted deformable-world simulator. Wax is a volume-conserving controlled fluid body, not a general Navier-Stokes solver. Gullet moves authored structural chunks. Root follows a sampled curve through declared wet substrate. Inside-Out uses explicit complementary topology; arbitrary unmarked artwork does not become collision automatically. Stitch is constrained to compatible seams. Shadow traversal follows a generated bounded silhouette domain. These bounds keep the physical rules inspectable and repeatable.

Temporal Echo bodies remain independently collidable with terrain/props but ignore their original actor, avoiding overlapping self-spawn collisions; Mirror twins do collide with each other. This is an explicit implementation difference from the atlas's possible self-collision cost. No claim is made that every incidental micro-story or every suggested animation is a literal cinematic reconstruction.

The presentation is original compact pixel art, procedural animation, typed dialogue/captions and an offline synthesized score. It does not reproduce reference artists' images or Nintendo art/audio. It establishes playable identity and readable state; human judgement of horror effectiveness, aesthetics, difficulty, pacing, ergonomics and controller feel is still a manual check. A complete scripted campaign does not establish commercial production quality.

The separate foundation retains its own original-before-atlas history and native acceptance. The source, test inputs and build are ordinary files and Unity objects; no app-only document runtime or AI model is required.
