# Q9B actual visual review

The new rail housings and girder tracks are attached to real RailPath spans and remove the anonymous oversized end gears. Actual Laundry captures show hanging garments and a more readable winch/track relationship. This is new native rendering, not concept imagery.

Two defects were rejected before the next candidate: (1) the new support brace was too solid/triangular and suggested a fake platform volume; it now has a transparent structural aperture. (2)10-metre room panels overlaid by the older miniature apartment fronts made the City crowded and scale-inconsistent. The construction modules are capped at7metres and the replaced owner's obsolete miniature front/window renderers are hidden. The visible floor/collider boundary remains unchanged. The original Q9B images remain intact.

Q9C includes new motion import/pose/stride checks, whole25stage off-camera construction checks and two additional H/I support-derived viewpoints per stage. Those additions remain unverified until the native results are read; scripts are not self-certification. The concept/extreme-quality gate remains open.
