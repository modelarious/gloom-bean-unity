# Q3 actual evidence and Q4 authoring corrections

125 native captures cover25 stages including original A/B/C and incoming D/E validation. All Q eligible surfaces have attached artwork and zero added colliders. This does not meet the concept quality bar: a side-by-side of the actual Laundry image and the concept reduced to the same240x160 shows weaker physical construction, local motifs and lighting in the game.

Two general implementation flaws matter more than another filter. Prop fitting distorted tall shrines and broad halos. Elevated-object dressing excluded every kinematic body, including the Laundry's whole hanging room chunks. Q4 preserves each prop's source aspect ratio and applies room fittings to appropriate static AND kinematic room surfaces, excluding free magnetic bodies, edible chunks and procession carriers. No sampled camera coordinates or screenshot IDs enter those selectors.

Full atlas reread also found incorrect thematic substitutions. Garden of No Seasons now receives a tree and actual four-season wheel rather than a book; Seamstress gets a sewing spindle; paired apartments get a facade with rooms; the actual ProcessionCarrier now carries three original pixel pallbearers. Their gait follows its measured Progress, not an unrelated free-running animation. A SeasonWheel view reads existing offset; it never changes the seasons.

The concept is a target and object-fragment source, not a completed scene. Posed native fixtures do not establish actual route success or animation feel. Q4 requires all125 matching captures, checks beyond the review cameras and actual gameplay regression before shipping.
