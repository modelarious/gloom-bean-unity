# Native Q5 review: keep improvements, reject material regressions

175/175 images captured without errors. All25 stages and50 genuinely unseen F/G positions examined; there are no quality-layer colliders and every eligible initial object is covered. This is coverage evidence, not a concept-quality pass.

The Laundry drums are now visible behind full moving rooms; the blue apartment interiors and quieter shadow planes make distant construction readable. The overbearing spotlight triangles are reduced. However Q5's foreground masonry replacement loses the better Q4 bevelled texture. Those three stone banks are rejected and restored from exact prior committed art. Q6 also replaces the flat bell/coffin motifs with bronze planes, engraving, strapping, worn timber and a relief face/hands. These update every matching stage, not selected samples.

The actual Q5 pictures still do not match the supplied concepts. Long runs of repeated fittings and weak expressive character motion remain. No strict fidelity or WL4-superiority pass is being recorded.
