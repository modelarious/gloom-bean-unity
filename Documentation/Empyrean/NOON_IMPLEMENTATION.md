# Noon Without Shadows — actual geometry
Original atlas p38: moving sunlight, cast silhouettes, overhead-noon Turn, deliberate magnetic-screen return. Implemented two suspended-saint shadow crossings. The Turn truly locks directional sunlight downwards and closes those shutters. Each must be opened again through a shadow made by its actual movable iron screen on a constrained rail; receiver provenance rejects a different caster or invalid cursor. The player pulls each screen with Lodestone forces and switches to Shadow to traverse that new silhouette. No direct gate grant appears in the witness. The own-body magnetic suspension Mercy is deliberately still unmet; the reserved high pickup is not a verified solution.
