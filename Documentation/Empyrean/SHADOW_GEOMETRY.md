# Shadow geometry groundwork
Actual collider outlines (including rotated rectangles and capsules) are projected using physics poses. Directional sunlight is the Minkowski sweep along a ray; point lamps use silhouette extrema. The convex-hull utility is not yet wired into the level in this commit. Bounded convex footprints are supported; arbitrary concave silhouettes are explicitly outside this first utility. New L18 routes must prove provenance of body-cast vs screen-cast bridges rather than merely touching a labeled receiver.

Integrated point and directional sunlight, filled silhouette rendering, source-collider provenance, and physically locked noon. Receivers now require a controlled valid shadow, so a stale cursor in newly lit space cannot press a latch. The body carries its ordinary attached shadow until I explicitly detaches it; after detachment the existing tether remains. Magnetic momentum continues when controlling the detached shadow, enabling the specified suspended-body puzzle. Component and live route verification are still pending for this commit.
